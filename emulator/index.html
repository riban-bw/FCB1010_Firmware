A CC on the pedal's channel that matches a stomp's number sets that stomp (64 and up is on) and lights its LED. Nothing is echoed back out.

How to use

  • Click or touch a footswitch. Keys 1–9, 0 are switches 1–10, ↑ and ↓ are UP and DOWN.
  • Hold a stomp for over 1s to see the momentary behaviour. Hold UP or DOWN for 400 ms to start the 4 Hz auto-repeat; the program change goes out on release.
  • For UP + DOWN together use the button under the unit, or hold both arrow keys.
  • Settings are saved in this browser, like the pedal's EEPROM. Power cycle reloads them.
